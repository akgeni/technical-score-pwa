Settings

Data comes from Yahoo Finance (free, no key, effectively unlimited) via a tiny relay you deploy yourself on Cloudflare Workers' free tier (100,000 requests/day, no card needed) — Yahoo's own response is missing a header browsers require, so this relay just adds it back. See cloudflare-worker/yahoo-proxy.js in the project repo for the ~1-minute copy-paste deploy steps. The URL is stored only on this device (localStorage); nothing goes anywhere except directly from your phone to your own Worker to Yahoo Finance.
